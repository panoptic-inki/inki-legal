2. Data We Collect

If you sign in with email and password, the password is handled and stored by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.

2.2 Preferences you give us during onboarding

  • Your native language and the language you are learning
  • Your app interface language
  • Your preferred news topics
  • How often you intend to read
  • Whether you want quizzes
  • Whether you were referred by another user, and that person's user ID
  • Whether you want to be discoverable by user ID in search

2.3 Learning and activity data

Created as you use the App:

  • Which articles you have opened and read, and when
  • How long you spent on an article (a fixed dwell threshold, not continuous tracking)
  • Your total number of articles read
  • Your daily reading streak and the date of your last qualifying read
  • Your "energy" balance and when it last changed
  • Your account plan, including the plan you had when you read an article (at present every account is on the Premium plan at no cost)

2.4 Social data

Only if you use the social features:

  • The users you follow and the users who follow you
  • Notifications generated for you (for example, "X started following you")
  • Your reading activity, which is shown in the feed of people who follow you (see section 4)
  • A daily counter of how many user-ID searches you have run, used only to enforce a rate limit against abuse

2.5 Data collected automatically by Google services

We do not collect these fields ourselves, but the Google services the App is built on do:

  • Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In record your IP address and device information when you sign in, for security and abuse prevention.
  • Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions process technical data such as your IP address and request logs when the App talks to our backend.

2.6 Data stored only on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Articles you download for offline reading, which are saved in the App's own private storage
  • Your notification and language preferences cached for offline use
  • A copy of your account data, kept by Firebase so the App works offline
  • Reminder notifications, which are scheduled locally by your device

2.7 Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect precise (GPS) location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files, SMS or call logs
  • We do not collect health, biometric, financial or other special-category data
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (see section 10)
  • We do not collect your advertising ID, and we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not collect payment information, because nothing can be bought in the App (see section 5)
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising

4. What Other Users Can See

Some of your data is visible to other people using the App. This is a feature of the App, not a disclosure to a third party:

  • Your username and profile picture are visible to users who follow you, who you follow, and who find you by user ID.
  • Your reading activity — the articles you read — appears in the feed of users who follow you, along with your name and picture.
  • Your streak count and total articles read are visible on your public profile within the App.
  • Your user ID can be shared by you (for example, as an invite code) and can be searched by other users.

You can stop appearing in user-ID search at any time: Settings → Privacy → Discoverable by user ID.

Your email address, your plan, your energy balance and your preferences are never shown to other users.

5. No Advertising and No Payments

The App currently shows no advertisements and offers no purchases or subscriptions. Every account gets all features for free. No advertising SDK collects data from you, and we process no payment data.

If we introduce advertising or paid features in the future, we will update this policy before doing so and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first (for example, before showing personalised ads).

8. How Long We Keep Data, and How to Delete It

We keep your personal data for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account in the App: go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the data associated with it — your user document, your reading history, your social connections, your notifications and your feed — as well as your sign-in credentials.

Requesting deletion without the App: you can also request deletion at https://zecbfernandes.github.io/news-app-legal/delete-account.html, or by emailing panoptic.general@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days.

What happens after deletion:

  • Your data is removed from our live database immediately.
  • Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.
  • We may retain a minimal record of the deletion, and any data we must keep by law.
  • Articles you downloaded for offline use are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Aggregated or anonymised statistics that cannot identify you may be kept.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected
  • Erasure — have your data deleted (see section 8)
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including profiling
  • Withdraw consent — for anything based on consent, such as reminder notifications, with no effect on processing carried out before you withdrew it

Most of this can be done in the App: you can edit your name, languages, topics, avatar and discoverability in Settings, turn reminders off with the Reminders switch, and delete your account under Account.

For anything else, email panoptic.general@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, CNPD — Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt), or to the authority in your own country.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. In the European Union, the minimum age for consenting to online services varies between 13 and 16 depending on the country; if you are below the age in your country, you need your parent's or guardian's permission to use the App.

If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact panoptic.general@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

11. Security

We protect your data with:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for everything the App sends
  • Encryption at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure
  • Firebase Security Rules that stop one user from reading or writing another user's private data
  • Server-side logic for operations that must not be controlled by the client
  • Password hashing and storage handled entirely by Firebase Authentication

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the CNPD within 72 hours and inform you without undue delay, as the GDPR requires.
